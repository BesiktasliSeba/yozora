AppleColorEmoji.ttf holds the pictures of every emoji, so pages show the same set as the exteria chats.
tools/make_emoji_font.py builds it from the emoji pack of ios/exteria (resources/emoji/emoji.pack): Apple's iOS 26.4
emoji (Unicode 17), reduced to 56 pixels, plus the 19 emoji of Unicode 18.0 that the font lacks as Noto Emoji 3D
